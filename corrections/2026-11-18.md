## What changed in the method

These describe current behaviour.

- Briefs no longer say a company will "raise soon". They are a ranked shortlist with checkable evidence, not predictions.
- New gates hold senior researchers (first paper more than about 15 years ago), existing founders (a PI on a company award, a company affiliation, or verified web evidence), federal agency names, and contract-R&D shops.
- A company scored below the base rate no longer passes on a fresh trigger.
- Every pick is hand-checked before it is sent.

## Limits of this check

- Most sources are web pages found by search. Some were opened. Aggregator figures (Caplight, VCBacked) are not confirmed by a press release or an SEC filing.
- Names were matched to public profiles by name and affiliation. The people were not asked to confirm.
- The AOSense link comes from a patent listing. The co-founding claim is unconfirmed.
- The expected values use stored estimates and the cards' decile rates. They are approximations.
